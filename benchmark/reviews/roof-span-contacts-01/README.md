# Fitting roof edges to visible supports

The previous pipeline at `99655be` produced the input builds. This challenger fits upper strips to photographed roof and wall masks. A proposed roof course requires two spatially separated columns with photographed vertical faces on two consecutive block levels. Candidate grid planes compete on how well their integer edges fit the perimeter and cover the observed mask. Each addition must retain original-image pixels on the nominated face after all placements are rendered together. The default candidate in this experiment has a mean edge residual of at most 0.14 blocks and a margin of at least 0.05 blocks over a competing plane. The broad candidate relaxes these to 0.19 and zero.

On 72 development photos, the guarded choice added nine correct placements and two extras, with no lost or hidden placements. The broad choice added ten correct placements and three extras. All nine guarded gains came from the high-right earth-palette Watchpost 1, and both extras from the high-right earth-palette Sawtooth 2. The guarded choice was frozen at `4e41f68` before testing other cohorts. [Per-photo scores and inputs](development.json)

An exchange tested at `fa71b5c` can remove an old block if its competing roof has a stronger atlas match over at least 70% of the old block's visible pixels. The method would restore the old block if its replacement failed the final render. Across development, 65 old cells overlapped proposed alternative cells on measured faces. Nineteen old cells had at least 70% pixel coverage. No more-distant alternative won a sufficiently stronger atlas match on the same pixels. The exchange produced the same result as the guarded choice on development, so it remains experimental.

Across all 72 development photos, the guarded rectangles nominated 122 absent cells. Only 25 corresponded to visible truth after applying the current build's best single translation. When all nominations competed with the current build, ten of these correct proposals had zero native pixels on their nominated face, and four more had fewer than 12 pixels. Eleven had at least 12. This diagnostic opens source truth only after the predictions are frozen. A wider generator or a second source of visible evidence is needed for bigger gains.
