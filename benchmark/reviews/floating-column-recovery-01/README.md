# Recovering the Viaduct's missing column

The far-left column was visible, detected and counted. The exporter kept the largest connected chart group and dropped its separate 24-cell chart. Attaching that chart recovered **all 24 positions without an extra block**. Correcting the second base and upper post grids brought Viaduct from **175 correct / 33 extras to 217 / 26**. Keep this experiment separate from main and floating integration. Only two of five proposed chart attachments were completely correct, and Viaduct supplied the only measured gain from the new post and base rules. ## Where the left column disappeared

The primary wall mask, mask-0, covers zero pixels on the 24 visible front faces of the left column. Its actual Surface Finder proposal, mask-1, covers **48,486 of 48,791 native face pixels**. Its initial atlas chart has 13 cells and the existing expanded chart has 24. All 24 expanded centers independently hit distinct frontmost native faces, comprising four stripped-wood fronts, 14 stone brick and six cobblestone. The paired prior export contains zero of these 24 registered coordinates.

The source grids share signed tangents. Projecting mask-1 into mask-0 yields integer tangent shift **(−15, −1)** with four neighboring pairs and 0.160-block maximum residual. The existing coplanar join rejects discrepancies over 0.13 block at its extreme samples and requires a maximum bidirectional reprojection error of 0.5 pixel. The latter would be 10.20 pixels here. The selected 208-block component contains masks 0, 2 and 3. Accurate mask-1 was the second component at 24 blocks. The downstream drop affects both the viewer and the actual decoded pack. ## What the source rules measured

1. **Chart attachment.** Look for a counted, disconnected fragment with the same oriented axes and normal as a selected chart. Require at least four cells, extent on both tangents, 90th-percentile phase error at most 0.30 cell, worst error at most 0.45, at least two adjoining grid edges and no more than 10% existing overlap. The geometric control attaches the proposed cells at zero relative depth.
2. **Repeated texture and depth.** Compare sampled source RGB tiles on corresponding rows of both charts. At least four child cells and two rows need evidence. A tile can qualify by centered image correlation and similar color, or by strong vertical grain on both tiles and a tighter color match. Matching tiles vote for the existing signed depth of that row on the selected parent. Three of the left column's four log tiles had correlations over 0.60 with chosen source examples. Vertical grain supplied another match with correlation 0.10. Disabling the grain shortcut yielded identical decoded scores because the other votes already established the row depth.
3. **Extent of the second brown base.** Mask-0 covers 96–100% of each of its four native front faces. The inherited chart centers hit only the two right faces. For small existing depth patches with same-color peers at the same source row, follow connected, color-compatible native pixels around the current patch. Estimate face count from peer widths and fit the local horizontal edges. Keep a candidate only with majority visible patch support. The second base grew from two proposals to four. Both new positions were correct. The other two measured base fronts retained four cells each.
4. **Skyline post fronts.** Above a long projecting band, look for repeated, narrow upright protrusions with native supported pixels under bright blue sky. Require at least three crests and repeated integer spacing. The crest establishes each local top, width and horizontal phase. Limit the post's height to the measured gap down to the band shoulder and resample each cell on the source mask. All eight Viaduct posts yielded two cells. Placing them on the parent plane added nine extras and no correct positions. Assigning the same local grids the projecting band's +1 offset recovered 16 correct positions and removed seven extras. Each fitted face's material is ranked from its visible source texels.

The bounded native diagnostic retains the same source attachment candidates and tangent translations. It permits a normal shift of at most two cells per proposal and omission. At least five of nine inset samples must agree on one native frontmost face of the expected orientation. The shifted voxel must match that face's registered world coordinate under the prior whole-build alignment. This diagnostic supplies no additional tangent positions or chart candidates. It reaches all 24 Viaduct child faces.
