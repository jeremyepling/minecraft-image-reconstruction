Giving the Viaduct's three sampled brown bases a separate one-block depth increased correct placements from 170 to 175 and reduced extras from 41 to 33, including the concurrent upper-band refit. **Source evidence.** Each connected source depth component gets its own homography. The fit searches tangent pitch from 0.85 to 1.15 of the parent pitch and phase within ±0.375 parent cells about the patch median, separately on each axis. It preserves the parent's vanishing directions. The source objective scores directional RGB transitions at expected edges against quarter-cell guard samples, requiring evidence across at least three grid lines. It retains the parent setting on an axis with weak evidence. Native pixel centers inside the previously proposed patch cells and the original image mask bound resampling. A proposed face needs more than half of 81 inset samples on those visible pixels and a shortest edge of at least 3.5 pixels. Its material is reranked from its masked source texels. The patch still uses the parent chart's integer tangent origin and source-signed normal displacement.

**Small-step source test.** On charts where the prior rule found a step, a local graph searches the remaining cells. It requires parallel source normals and a discontinuity in slope-corrected source inverse depth between a component of two to twelve cells and a component at least twice its size. It requires all accepted boundary contacts to agree in direction. A single contact can qualify a small patch. The rule uses no color or material class to assert depth. It found 24 additional small parts on the active conditions. The deliberately permissive contact rule produced false or harmful moves elsewhere.

**Signed boundary guard.** A separate source arm checks each original depth part at three locations along each shared edge, sampling 0.30 parent units into the two neighboring cells. At least two sample pairs must belong to the image mask. Both adjacent sides use the same robust planar slope and noise estimate. At least three contacts and two thirds of all comparable contacts must exceed the signed residual threshold. Rejected parts return to the parent plane. A chart with no surviving original part keeps its smaller regions on the parent plane too. Mill passed 16 of 22 contacts, Viaduct 26 of 26, and Castle 12 of 21. Across 84 it accepted 19 of 26 prior parts. Outside the three reviewed layouts this guard lost 17 correct placements and added 11 extras versus prior source depth. Harbor lost 11 correct and Arcbridge lost nine.

**Native diagnostic.** The bounded reference arm provides the subpixel corners of a complete, frontmost, parallel full-cube face inside the source patch. It checks the face ID at interior native pixels, native visible counts and corner reprojection. It supplies no reference material, absolute block position, extent or integer chart attachment. It retains inherited sampling when a face is unavailable. Eighteen of 26 existing active patches had an anchor. A perfect corner measurement on a mixed or truncated source patch can still worsen the final export. The chooser compares whole prior, source-refit, source-refit-with-small-steps, signed-boundary-guard and native-face-refit exports per condition, using recorded strict F1 with ties favoring prior. This diagnostic describes only these offered outputs.
