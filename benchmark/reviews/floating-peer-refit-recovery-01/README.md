Two recalibrations at different source resolutions supply the evidence. A complete tile must pass the opaque 26.3 full-cube atlas and quarter-phase test on the new ruler. Each new cell also samples 81 source pixels and reprojects them through the old ruler. A vote uses covered subcells with correlation of at least .30 and per-channel color distance no greater than 23 from the local median. Confidence-squared weighted votes require total weight four, a .45 share and a 1.5× lead. Neighboring cells whose color quantiles resemble the calibrated tile under bounded exposure form four-neighbor components. A component can use the verified material when it contains that tile or an independent local subcell consensus for the same material. Other cells keep the overlap result. No predicted support is added. The packed input and output coordinate lists are checked for exact equality in local and registered space.
