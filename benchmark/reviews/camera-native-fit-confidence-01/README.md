# Confidence from repeatable native pavement pixels

Keep `experiment/camera-native-fit-confidence` experimental. The learned fallback adds one development camera beyond the frozen, combined native boundary and GeoCalib proposals. It adds zero Harbor or Castle cameras. The additional Pillars export recovers 323 visible positions, including 255 exact block IDs, and adds 551 extras. The source rule froze at `38eb566` before applying it to the other buildings. Those buildings are previously reviewed regression families.

## What the model learned

The gate runs only on poses the frozen native renderer numerically refined after both its ordinary source arms declined the image. Seven continuous features describe a candidate pose. Three summarize the four source atlas correlations. Four compare photographed pixels to other pixels displaced by one or more complete pavement units under that pose, contrast full and half-unit displacements, and measure consistency across near and far bands. The upper band reaches above the area used by the atlas verifier. A known camera, building name, palette, capture configuration or sibling angle never enters the feature vector. One source image supplies all pixels.

Camera labels enter only during fitting and grading. A positive has 90th-percentile error within two native pixels on disjoint visible corners after one integer XYZ translation. Approximate duplicates within a frame are removed by rounding its feature coordinates to two decimals. Each training frame has equal total fit weight. The logistic regression uses fixed L2 regularization on standardized features. Leaving out each of the six building families in turn keeps all layouts, viewpoints, palettes and candidate variants from that family together. The threshold is the greater of 0.90 and the highest out-of-fold incorrect or calibration-negative probability plus 0.02.

The 49 development frames supplied 72 retained feature vectors, 55 accurate and 17 inaccurate. The probability threshold stayed at 0.90. Out-of-family predictions exceeded it on 54 accurate candidates and zero inaccurate ones. Seven frozen negative fixtures remained abstentions; five had no eligible pose, and the two phase-discontinuous fixtures had candidates whose maximum confidence was 0.671. These fixtures participated in threshold calibration. ## What transferred

Pillars 03 marble rear-oblique received 0.928 confidence and selected the second source-refined boundary proposal. Its disjoint-corner P90 was 0.47 native pixels. The actual exported pack contained 323 correct positions, 1,318 misses and 551 extras. This exact matrix was also selected by the separate diagnostic grader and verified in the ordinary build manifest.

All five Harbor failures already had a camera from the frozen native method. The four remaining Castle views had maximum model probabilities of 0.070, 0.157, 0.078 and 0.352. Two Castle rear-view proposals were physically accurate after relative translation but did not meet the source threshold. Supplying those two using reference answers gained 3,779 correct positions and 1,289 extras. Most of the measurable remaining opportunity belongs to one reviewed Castle viewpoint shared by two palettes.
