A block neighboring a visible wall or post can nominate a vertical face. The candidate must win actual native pixels with matching detected orientation. Its photographed texels are compared to the calibrated atlas, two disjoint sets of texels, shifted texture positions and two nearby depths. The classifier uses both the exposed texture and scene evidence, including palette matches elsewhere on the same plane and whether another face of this proposed cube projects outside the observed silhouette. Candidates compete together for the same rays; a candidate losing its witness is removed. The final check protects the last visible pixel of each existing block.

Each development comparison used the 72 whole-build training screenshots from Visible Builds v2. The fixed texture rules gave 89 correct for 12 extras (strict), 151 for 34 (balanced) and 193 for 119 (wide). With one entire building family held out per fit, the logistic model at probability .65 recovered 152 for nine extras; .80 recovered 135 for four; .90 recovered 85 for three. We froze **.80** to retain broad recovery with fewer hidden errors. The final model uses all six training families. [Family measurements](development.json)
