# Reconstruction adjustments and lessons for training

These entries cover model inputs, rules around model outputs, and evaluation changes. **Shipped** means ordinary builds use the adjustment. **Experiment** means it was tested and left out of the default. **Diagnostic** means we learned something about errors without changing predictions.

### November 26, 2026

- **Experiment — trim thin extensions attached to a broader surface.** On Courtyard B, both rules removed 208 incorrect pixels and kept the genuine 1 × 4 Shopfront B face intact. Requiring color agreement with the surroundings kept all 517 previous surface matches and added one, but removed 587 correct pixels from another Courtyard wall. Keep the current Surface Finder. Include genuine thin extensions attached to broader walls as training controls alongside standalone thin faces. [Scores and close-ups](benchmark/ATTACHED_STRIP.md)

- **Experiment — expand the crop to the detected surface.** Of 456 usable crops, 67 missed more than a tenth of the detected outline. Full expansion reduced that to zero, and guarded expansion reduced it to two. Exact block sizes fell from 94 to 90 and 93 respectively. Neither recovered a new exact size. Keep the existing crop for counting. Track window coverage separately from the ability to count texture repeats. [Scores and sheet](benchmark/CROP_REVIEW.md#crop-expansion)

- **Shipped — group connected visible block faces.** On counts builds, the Block Grouper links faces that form a convex cube corner. Blocker Picker reads the widest eligible face once and propagates the block ID to its linked faces. With recorded corners, all 198 true pairs were joined without a wrong join. The automatic grid had no complete face pairs in this test. Include observed convex and recessed corners when evaluating grouping. [Replay and examples](benchmark/reviews/blocker-picker-groups-01/README.md) [Selected-face matcher comparison](benchmark/reviews/blocker-picker-sky-groups-01/README.md)

- **Shipped — recognize sky through enclosed openings.** Jeremy confirmed the highlighted pixels in Crossing and Galleries A–D were sky. Compare each opening with open sky at the same image height. For a flat patch of color, also look for the distant ground at the same horizon. On the 56 saved views this removed all 1,301 highlighted pixels, reduced sky still claimed as surface from 1,605 pixels to nine, and kept all 517 surface matches. On 72 further labeled images the rule marked zero additional building pixels. Packed ice has texture pixels close to the reviewed blue, so retain a material control when expanding to other lighting. Correct block sizes stayed at 94; one previously unresolved size became an incorrect estimate. Ordinary builds apply the same-row check after exterior-connected sky removal and before crops and counts. [Feedback](benchmark/reviews/sky-ambiguity-01/feedback.jsonl) · [Same openings before and after](benchmark/reviews/sky-gap-01/comparison.png) · [Block texture check](benchmark/reviews/sky-gap-atlas-01/report.json) · [72-image control](benchmark/reviews/sky-gap-control-01/report.json)

- **Shipped — keep clouds out of the building.** After the finder proposes surfaces, a color-and-connectivity check removes sky and cloud pixels joined to a clear-noon sky gradient. On Workshop, a whole 26,988-pixel “surface” disappeared. On Crossing A, the filter trimmed 1,010 sky pixels and kept the entire wall. Across 56 views, matches went from 516 to 517 and extra guesses fell from 204 to 173. All 63,279 pixels in an independent cloud-color check were removed. Nine removed pixels overlapped labeled building faces, and 804 independently identified blue pixels survived in enclosed openings. For training, include detached clouds, clouds touching rooflines, blue sky through holes, and pale blocks as separate examples. This was an output filter; the model weights stayed fixed. [Before and after](benchmark/reviews/sky-control-01/examples.jpg) · [Per-picture scores](benchmark/reviews/sky-01/pictures.md)

- **Evaluation — visible evidence only.** The shared training dataset records which faces and blocks the supplied camera can see. Train and score against those visible labels. Keep clear sightlines and unknown space distinct, especially through doorways and between pillars. [Product intent](PRODUCT_INTENT.md) · [152-image dataset](training/DATASET.md)
- **Diagnostic — distinguish a bad outline from a bad crop.** Of 516 matched surfaces, 505 produced a usable face-on crop. On 104 of those, the four-corner sampling window left over 10% of the finder's own pixels outside. The review now shows estimated and actual block dimensions together. For training, grade the mask, sampling window and block count separately. The subsequent crop-expansion experiment tested that idea. [Examples and measurements](benchmark/CROP_REVIEW.md)

### November 25, 2026

- **Shipped — zoom in to separate walls.** Pixel-wise Planarity sees nine closer views of the same screenshot. Close-ups may divide an existing surface while preserving its footprint and holes. This kept all 513 previous matches and added three, with the same opening coverage. For training, include narrow side faces at different image scales and check the gaps after combining predictions. [Split-only zoom](benchmark/CROP_SPLIT.md)
- **Experiment — confirm a cut inside one wall with depth or direction.** This recovered a 1 × 5 Portico face and got its dimensions right. It produced six extra guesses and missed the Workshop ledge twice. Score unnecessary fragments as well as new matches. [One-wall experiment](benchmark/CONTAINED.md)
- **Experiment — accept additional outlines from closer views.** Extra regions found zero additional matching surfaces. In one Registry view an addition covered 260 open-floor samples. Use exposed floor and the spaces between walls as background examples. [Close-up additions and protected edges](benchmark/GUARD_ZOOM.md)
- **Experiment — require two models to agree on a plane outline.** Agreement between MobileSAM and Planarity recovered walls, but sometimes cut through a neighboring wall on Crossing. Even the later edge safeguard left one previously matched wall below threshold. Check which individual surfaces were gained and lost. [Agreement test](benchmark/PLANE_ACCEPTANCE.md) · [Crossing close-up](benchmark/GUARD_ZOOM.md)
- **Shipped — look for missed planes after the first pass.** Adding full-image Pixel-wise Planarity to Wall Recovery raised matches from 501 to 513 and kept every earlier match. Measure how often the raw model proposed the right plane and how often the final finder retained it. [Plane recovery](benchmark/AFTERPASS_MODELS.md)
- **Shipped — preserve outlines, holes and disconnected surfaces.** Regions First uses MoGe-2 surface directions and relative depth. The perimeter follows the owned pixels so a wall can wrap around an opening. Four corners are used later for sampling. Train and evaluate on visible masks, including the narrow underside of an overhang. [Mask comparison](benchmark/MASK_ROUND.md) · [Perimeter review](benchmark/PERIMETER_ROUND.md)
